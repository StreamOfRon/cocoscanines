Services

Daycare

Full-day supervised play in our home environment. We cater to each individual dog’s needs with socialization, exercise, and plenty of love.

Hours: Monday–Friday, 7am–6pm

What to expect:

  • Supervised group play with compatible dogs
  • Individual attention and care
  • Indoor and outdoor play areas
  • Rest periods throughout the day
  • Daily updates for pet parents

Boarding

Overnight stays in our home, not a kennel. Your dog gets daily walks, individual attention, and a report card so you never miss a moment. Hours: Drop-off by 5pm, pickup by 5pm next day

What to expect:

  • Comfortable home environment
  • Daily walks and outdoor time
  • Individual attention and cuddles
  • Daily report cards with photos
  • Medication administration if needed

Walking

Individual and group walks tailored to your dog’s energy level and needs. Fresh air and exercise in the beautiful Pacific Northwest.

Walk options:

  • Individual walks (30 or 60 minutes)
  • Small group walks with compatible dogs
  • Adventure hikes on local trails

What to Bring

For daycare or boarding, please bring:

  • Your dog’s regular food (pre-portioned in bags)
  • Any medications with instructions
  • A favorite toy or blanket (optional)
  • Vaccination records (required for all services)
  • Emergency contact information

Pricing

Contact us for current pricing and to schedule a meet & greet. We offer competitive rates and packages for regular clients.
